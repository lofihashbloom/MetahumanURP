### Taro (Epic MetaHuman)

What is included: the exported meshes, textures (converted from TGA to PNG without loss), the DNA behaviour stream, hair input tables and material parameters (`Source`, `Character`), and data derived from them (`Generated`): strand meshes baked from the exported groom curves, groom-to-skin bindings, and hair shadow fields.

Changes made for this repository: format conversion, strand baking, a painted face colour map and teeth maps derived from the exported teeth maps, and the lip-sync and head-motion track in `Rendering/Photo` that drives the rig. The character itself (mesh, rig, hair) is Epic's.

### Jacket (Unity Time Ghost)

Only the torso and sleeves of the Time Ghost hero outfit are used. They were refitted to Taro's body, cut at the waist, rebuilt with an opened collar, new zipper, piping and lining geometry, and re-skinned to the MetaHuman skeleton. The hands, gloves, boots, hood, helmet, mask and face of the original are not included. No original Time Ghost texture file is included: the leather and zipper maps in `Clothes/Source` were generated by script from the outfit's UV layout, normal map and wear mask. The result is a heavily modified derivative of the Time Ghost suit, and it is credited here because it is derived from it.

### Speech recording

The video in `Media/Taro-talking.mp4` and its preview contain this recording and the sample scene as rendered; they follow the same terms as their parts.

The talking-shot line was synthesised from a text written for this sample. The track that drives the face (`Rendering/Photo`) was derived from that audio.
